Controls

Super Star Car-style keyboard controls: WASD / arrows to drive. On the grid, hold W to rev to the launch rpm; launch control holds the car and releases it automatically when the red lights go out. Steer into the marked pit entry and cross the gold gate for automatic braking and control, choose tyres and fuel when you stop in one of the four shared boxes, and resume driving at pit exit. Gamepad: left stick steer, RT throttle, LT brake, Start pause.
