Controls

Super Star Car-style keyboard controls: WASD / arrows to drive. On the grid, hold W to rev to the launch rpm; launch control holds the car and releases it automatically when the red lights go out. Steer into the pit entry for automatic control (or press P to let the autopilot take it), choose tyres and fuel when you stop in one of the four shared boxes, and resume driving at pit exit. Press G for DRS inside a zone when eligible. C changes camera. Gamepad: left stick steer, RT throttle, LT brake, X DRS, Y camera, Start pause.
