# Circuit data: sources, licences and accuracy

The six circuits in `index.html` (the `CIRCUIT_DATA` block between the `==CIRCUIT-DATA==` markers) are generated by
`tools/build-circuits.cjs` from published, reusable datasets. Nothing in the block was drawn by hand. This page lists
the sources and their licences, says what was measured and what was estimated, and gives the numbers the build
printed.

## Build steps (tools/build-circuits.cjs)

1. Download the TUM CSVs (x, y, width right, width left), the bacinger GeoJSON and the SRTM tiles (cached).
2. Fit each TUM line to the bacinger trace by a rigid iterative-closest-point fit (rotation and translation, no
   scaling). The residual is an independent check of the two traces against each other.
3. Move the centre line to the middle between the two edges and smooth the widths.
4. Sample the DEM along the line, median-filter (9 samples) and smooth (per-circuit window, below), because SRTM is a
   surface model that includes trees and buildings.
5. Suzuka only: lift the upper road of the figure-of-eight crossing to a 7.5 m clearance, over 170 m ramps.
6. Rotate the line to start at the start/finish line, scale it to the published lap length, and encode it.

## Estimated, not measured

- **Track width.** The game uses a minimum width of 10 m (`MIN_HALF_W`): where the TUM width is narrower (Monza,
  Spa, Suzuka, Interlagos in places) the road is widened symmetrically to 10 m. The satellite widths themselves are
  TUM's measurement and vary in quality by location (their README says so).
- **Start/finish line positions** are estimates read off the corner layout: neither dataset contains them. Positions
  along the TUM line: Monza 315 m, Silverstone 90 m, Spa 140 m, Suzuka 235 m, Bahrain 170 m, Interlagos 30 m.
- **Pit lanes.** The game's pit lane is a lateral lane beside the main straight (on the real side: the right in the
  racing direction everywhere except Interlagos, where it is on the left). Real entries and exits that leave or join
  elsewhere (Silverstone, Spa, Interlagos) are not reproduced; entry and exit points are placed within the main
  straight.
- **Suzuka overpass height.** The DEM gives the natural height difference at the crossing (1.8 m); the remaining
  5.7 m of the 7.5 m clearance is added to the upper road over 170 m ramps. The real bridge's clearance is not in any
  of the sources.
- **Elevation detail.** SRTM is about 30 m resolution and includes canopy and buildings. Short features (kerb-level
  crests, compressions shorter than about 50-100 m) are lost in the smoothing; the overall climbs and descents are
  kept.
- **Corner names** are placed from the geometry (the nearest detected bend to each named corner's position along the
  lap); Bahrain uses turn numbers.
- **Kerbs, run-off, gravel, barriers, grandstands and scenery** are generated from each circuit's definition and
  environment style, not from survey data.
